# Skate Lodge

A platform for figure skaters, their coaches, and their clubs. Its skaters are mostly adults, with some of 14 to 17.

**Status: planning.** Nothing is built yet. This repository holds the plan and the record of decisions so far.

## The idea

Skate Lodge grows out of [MySkate](https://github.com/iiSurf/MySkate), a practice tracker for figure skaters. The plan is one product with six parts:

- **The platform.** Accounts, sync across devices, and clubs with a coach view: a coach assigns homework and sees how their skaters are doing.
- **Jump Lab.** Film a jump on your phone and get measurements from the video: air time, height, rotations, and the landing, saved to that skill's history.
- **The game.** Rink time turned into a game you play off the ice: collectibles earned from sessions, a passport of stamps from the rinks you visit, levels that each unlock something, and a pond of your own to decorate.
- **The AI coach.** Carried over from [MySkate Coach](https://github.com/iiSurf/MySkate-Coach), to explain the numbers.
- **Friends and the rink map.** Find friends by username, and see on a map, drawn from above, which rinks they are skating at now. Skaters who want to meet new people can share where they are with everyone.
- **Rink schedules.** Several rinks' schedules on one page, with an alert when a session you picked moves or is cancelled. When a schedule has to be updated by hand, one skater's update, once confirmed, updates it for everyone.

Around those sit the things that make it fun to come back to: a scoreboard that reveals your numbers when you save a session, jump cards that show how each jump is done, a dress studio for your skater, seeing which clubmates are on the same ice, and more. The full list is in the roadmap.
